Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

The short version: The Prompt Vault is a free, static website with no accounts, no payments, and no tracking. We don't ask you to sign in, we don't run analytics or advertising cookies, and there's nothing for us to sell — because we collect essentially nothing about you.

Who we are

The Prompt Vault is published under the ProPrompt brand at https://getproprompt.com. Questions about privacy? Email hello@getproprompt.com — we read every message.

What we collect

From ordinary browsing of the site: nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts to create, no forms to fill, and no analytics or advertising trackers on the page.

  • No account, no login. The whole vault is free and anonymous — we never ask for your email or a password.
  • No analytics, no cookies, no cross-site tracking. We don't measure you, profile you, or follow you around the web.
  • Preferences stay on your device. A few settings — such as your saved favourites and theme — are kept in your browser's localStorage. This never leaves your device and we never receive it. You can clear it any time in your browser settings.

Hosting

The site is served as static files by our web host (GitHub Pages) with DNS provided by Cloudflare. Like any web host, their servers process basic technical request data (such as your IP address) for the sole purpose of delivering the page to your browser. We don't combine this with anything else or use it to identify you.

If you email us

If you choose to write to hello@getproprompt.com, we'll have your email address and whatever you put in your message — used only to reply to you.

Your rights

Because we don't hold personal information about you, there's very little to access, correct, or delete. If you've emailed us and want that correspondence removed, or you have any privacy question, contact hello@getproprompt.com and we'll sort it out. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

Children

The Prompt Vault is intended for a general, adult audience and is not directed to children under 16. Since we don't collect personal information, none is knowingly collected from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above shows when. Questions? hello@getproprompt.com.
